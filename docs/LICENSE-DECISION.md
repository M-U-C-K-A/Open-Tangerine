# License decision

Final user instruction: genuine open source, including permission for others to sell it.
Chosen license: GPL-3.0-only. Original project code is covered by NOTICE and LICENSE.

MIT and Apache-2.0 permit commercial use and proprietary derivatives. GPLv3 permits commercial use and sale but requires covered distributed derivatives to preserve GPL rights and provide corresponding source under its terms. AGPL adds a network interaction source condition for modified versions; it is not necessary for the first desktop-only scope. Noncommercial licenses are not OSI open source and were rejected by the user's latest instruction.

Syntax Error Private Limited retains rights in its own work; the license grants permissions rather than transferring ownership. Contributors retain their copyrights under the inbound GPL policy. Third-party libraries remain under their own licenses. An assignment would be a separate legal document, not something GitHub or GPL automatically does. No claim of exclusive ownership over all community or AI-generated code is made.

GPL grants for compliant recipients cannot simply be revoked later. Private modifications need not be public; distributed source need not be contributed back to this repository. Ordinary user-converted files do not become GPL merely through processing. Trademark permission is separate from source licensing. Brand clearance and chain-of-title review remain open.

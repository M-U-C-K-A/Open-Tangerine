What these numbers mean

ZIP counts are GitHub asset download requests across releases, including repeat downloads and testing. They do not measure unique people or installed apps. Source-code ZIP downloads are not included.

PostHog measures only visitors who allow analytics. Download clicks are intentions, not confirmed downloads. Browser privacy tools and missing referrers can limit attribution. Repository traffic covers a rolling 14-day window and can be delayed. No analytics are collected by this activity page or by the Mac app.
